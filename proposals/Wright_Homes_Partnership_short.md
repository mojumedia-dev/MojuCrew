Same investment as a traditional consultancy partnership. Materially different leverage.

This engagement is the foundation. The same AI staffing model expands cleanly into other areas of Wright's business as opportunities surface, including post-sale customer experience, warranty and service follow-up, vendor and trade communication, and back-office workflows. The marketing engagement is where the ROI is fastest to prove; the runway from there is open.

## What's Happening in Wright's Funnel

From Wright's own Google Analytics, January through April 2026:

- **Site users dropped 46%** (18,824 to 10,241) while engagement time went **up 27%**. Fewer visitors, higher quality. The structural problem is paid-channel decay: cross-network down 82%, paid social down 61%, organic flat.
- **Chat used by only 0.1% of visitors** (11 sessions on 10,241 in April). Phone clicks flat at ~45/month. Most engagement is being absorbed by individual agent pages (3,182 in April), routing buyers around a brand-owned funnel.
- **Custom Home page collapsed 94%** (2,928 to 162). Likely broken or demoted. Structural funnel leak.
- **Contact forms grew 28x** (19 to 547). Lead capture is working better; the gap is what happens after capture.
- **Offers Page up 77%** (2,543 to 4,510). Buyers are price-sensitive in this market.

Wright's biggest single conversion gap is appointment-to-sale at 11%, vs. industry benchmark 17 to 20%. Doubling that one number alone is worth ~50 additional homes per year, roughly **$32M in incremental revenue** at the $649K average sales price. AI continuously optimizing lead-response and follow-up is precisely how that gap closes.

## Reallocation, Not a Topline Increase

Wright's $500K budget contains roughly $80 to $90K per year of currently low-ROI spend that AI can redirect inside the first 60 days, before any conversation about increasing the topline:

- **Billboards**: $5,000/month with no measurable conversion attribution
- **Print**: ~$2,700/month, also unattributable in a digital-first builder funnel
- **No paid third-party listings** ($0 on Zillow / Realtor.com paid placement) while competitors are paying for top-of-listing position
- **Cross-network spend collapsed 82%** mid-quarter, needs immediate audit
- **Paid Social down 61%** while audience quality improved, smart bid expansion can recover volume

A continuously-optimizing AI doesn't audit waste once. It identifies inefficiency every hour, in real time, and reallocates the freed dollars into channels that convert.
